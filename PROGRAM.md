# FORGE: 12 weeks of strength + physique

**Priorities, in order:**
1. Weighted pull-up → back to +70 kg and beyond
2. Weighted dip → back to +80 kg and beyond
3. Freestanding HSPU and the 90° push-up
4. Physique: bigger back, arms, chest and shoulders, during a lean bulk from 70 to ~75 kg

**Start (Oct 2026):** 70 kg, 175 cm. Pull-up 1RM +40, dip 1RM +60, a few freestanding HSPU.

---

## The week

Every day starts with the main lift or skill, done fresh. The physique work comes after. Both chest days (D3 and D6) always include all three chest exercises: dip (lower), incline DB press (upper) and dumbbell fly (middle and stretch). Only the dip is heavy. The other two are hypertrophy work.

| Day | Session | Exercises |
|---|---|---|
| **D1** | Heavy Pull + Back & Biceps | **Weighted pull-up (heavy)**, barbell row 2×8–12 @60 kg (close to failure), lat pulldown 3×8–12, preacher curl 3×8–12, face pull 2×15–25, hanging leg raise 3×8–15, easy run |
| **D2** | Handstand + Shoulders | In this order, hardest skills first: **freestanding handstand hold 5×**, **90° negatives 4×2–5**, **freestanding HSPU 5×1–5**, **deficit wall HSPU 4×3–8**, easy run |
| **D3** | Heavy Dip + Chest, Triceps, Legs | **Weighted dip (heavy, lower chest)**, incline DB press 3×8–12 (upper chest), dumbbell fly 3×8–12 (middle chest + stretch), overhead triceps extension 3×10–15, Romanian deadlift 3×8–12, hanging leg raise 3×8–15, easy run |
| **D4** | Recovery | Longest easy run, mobility 10 min, light handstand holds |
| **D5** | Volume Pull + Arms | **Weighted pull-up (volume)**, wall HSPU 3×4–10, single-arm DB row 3×8–12, hammer curl 3×8–12, easy run |
| **D6** | Volume Dip + Chest & Legs | **Weighted dip (volume, lower chest)**, **90° negatives 4×2–5**, incline DB press 3×8–12 (upper chest), dumbbell fly 3×8–12 (middle chest + stretch), single-leg press 3×8–12, calf raise 3×10–15, easy run + strides |
| **D7** | Rest | Optional 20 min easy run, mobility |

The app runs the days in order, not by calendar date. Miss a day and you continue where you left off.

### Weekly hard sets per muscle

Indirect work counts as half a set: a set of dips counts as ½ a triceps set. That's the counting method that fit the data best in Pelland et al.

| Muscle | Direct | + indirect | Total | Research range for growth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lats / upper back | 9 pull-up + ~8 rows/pulldowns | (n/a) | **~17** | 12–20+ ✓ |
| Biceps | 6 curls (preacher, hammer) | ~9 from pulling | **~15** | ✓ |
| Chest | 9 dips + 6 incline/fly | (n/a) | **~15** | ✓ |
| Triceps | 3 overhead ext. | ~16 from dips/HSPU/press | **~19** | ✓ |
| Side delts | none (lateral raises removed) | ~10 from HSPU/press | **~10** | below range — see note |
| Rear delts | 4 face pulls | ~4 from rows | **~8** | maintenance+ |
| Hamstrings / glutes | 3 RDL | (n/a) | **~3** + running | moderate (intentional) |
| Quads | 6 single-leg press | (n/a) | **6** + daily running | moderate (intentional) |
| Calves | 3 | (n/a) | 3 + running | moderate |

Legs are deliberately moderate. Daily running already loads them, and heavy leg volume close to running is where the interference between running and lifting actually shows up. Upper-body muscles sit in the growth range.

## Secondary (physique) exercise rule
Every physique accessory is either **2 sets close to failure (RPE 9, ~10 reps)** or **3 sets close to failure (RPE 9, weight chosen so the last rep is hard)**. Deload weeks drop one RPE point; peak weeks (9–11) drop one set, which leaves 2 sets.

## Blocks

| Weeks | Block | Heavy day | Volume day | Physique accessories |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Rebuild | 5×3 @ RPE 7 → 8 → 8.5 | 4×6 @ 7 → 8 | full sets |
| 4 | Deload | 1 single @8 + 2×3 @7 | 3×5 @6 | −1 set, RPE −1 |
| 5–7 | Strength + Size | top single @8–9 + 3–4×2–3 back-offs | 4–5×4–5 @7.5–8 | full sets |
| 8 | Deload | 1 single @8 + 2×3 @7 | 3×4 @6 | −1 set, RPE −1 |
| 9–11 | Peak | top single @8.5–9 + doubles/singles | 3–4×3–4 @7.5–8 | **−1 set** to save recovery for heavy singles |
| 12 | Test | work up to a max on pull-up and dip | light | −1 set, RPE −1 |

After week 12 the cycle restarts from your new maxes.

**Runs:** 20 min in weeks 1–2, 25 min in weeks 3–5, 30 min from week 6, always easy. D4 is +5 min. D6 adds relaxed strides from week 3.

## How the coach adjusts

**Main lifts (pull-up, dip)**
- Every logged set (bodyweight + added kg, reps, RPE) re-estimates your max using the RTS RPE chart. Next session's weights come from that estimate.
- Went harder than planned (≥1.5 RPE over target, or missed reps)? The next session for that lift is 1 set shorter at RPE −0.5.
- Mid-workout, a set that's ≥1 RPE off target retunes the remaining sets.
- A bad day only half-counts. A good day can raise your estimate by up to 4% per session.

**Physique accessories (double progression)**
- Every exercise has a rep range, e.g. 8–12 at RPE 8.
- Hit the target reps on all sets at or under the RPE: next time +1 rep per set.
- Reach the top of the range: the weight goes up (2.5 kg on barbells and machines, 1–2 kg on dumbbells) and reps reset to the bottom.
- If you clearly did more reps than prescribed, the target jumps straight to what you did.
- **The first time you do an exercise, type the weight you actually used.** The app's starting weights are guesses.

**Bodyweight**
- Weigh in 2–3× a week on the Today screen.
- The main-lift loads automatically use your new bodyweight. As you get heavier, the added kg for the same effort drops a little. That's correct, because you're moving more total weight.

## Why it's built this way (evidence)

| Decision | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Main lifts at RPE 7–9, never to failure (except test week) | Robinson et al. 2024 (*Sports Medicine*, 55 studies): proximity to failure **doesn't** change strength gains. Strength work 3–5 reps short of failure is enough. |
| Physique accessories at RPE 8–9, isolation near failure | The same meta-analysis found **closer to failure = more growth**, suggesting 0–5 reps in reserve for hypertrophy. |
| ~12–20 weekly hard sets per upper-body muscle | Pelland et al. (*Sports Medicine*, 67 studies): more weekly volume = more growth with gradual diminishing returns (6→12→18→24 sets all add something). Strength flattens around ~15 direct sets, so pull-ups and dips stay at ~9 heavy sets each and the extra work is accessories. |
| Each lift and muscle trained 2×/week | Pelland et al.: frequency clearly helps **strength** (1→2×/week is the biggest jump). For growth, weekly volume matters more than how it's split. |
| Long rest on main lifts (3–4 min), 1–2 min on isolation | Schoenfeld et al. 2016: 3-min rest beat 1-min for both strength and size in trained men. |
| Overhead triceps extension, not pushdowns | Maeo et al. 2022: overhead extensions grew the triceps ~40% more than pushdowns over 12 weeks. The long head is stretched overhead. |
| Exercises with a deep stretch (preacher curl, incline press, RDL, fly, pulldown) | Training at long muscle lengths is linked to more growth, e.g. preacher curls grew the lower biceps (Zabaleta-Korta et al. 2023). The evidence is still developing, so treat it as a tiebreaker, not a law. |
| RPE autoregulation instead of fixed % | Helms et al. 2018 and Graham & Cleather 2019: autoregulated loading matched or beat fixed percentages for 1RM gains in trained lifters. |
| Daily easy runs | Schumann 2022 and Huiberts 2023 meta-analyses: essentially no interference for upper-body strength or growth. Interference shows up in lower-body strength and explosive power, so runs stay easy and legs moderate. |
| Comeback speed | Previously trained muscle keeps extra myonuclei and an "epigenetic memory" (Seaborne et al. 2018), so regaining +70/+80 should be faster than building it the first time. |

## Bulk (70 → ~75 kg)

From the off-season bodybuilding review by Iraki et al. 2019:

- **Rate of gain:** 0.25–0.5% of bodyweight per week, which is **~0.2–0.35 kg/week** for you. 75 kg is roughly 4–6 months away at that pace. The Today screen tracks this and tells you to add or cut ~200 kcal.
- **Surplus:** about 10–20% above maintenance. Remember that 25–35 min of daily running burns roughly 250–400 kcal extra, so eat for it.
- **Protein:** 1.6–2.2 g/kg/day, which is ~115–155 g at 70 kg, spread over 3–5 meals of ~0.4–0.55 g/kg each.
- **Fat:** 0.5–1.5 g/kg/day. **Carbs:** the rest, at least 3–5 g/kg/day, because running plus lifting needs them.

## Rules that keep you healthy

- Main lifts: leave reps in the tank. RPE 10 is for test week only.
- If an elbow or shoulder hurts (pain, not effort), do that day's main lift as the volume version at RPE 6 and pick "Very sore" or "Bad" in the check-in.
- Sleep 7–9 h. It's when both strength and muscle are actually built.
